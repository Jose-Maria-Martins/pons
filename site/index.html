“Why am I tired after a full night’s sleep?”

Pons read last night

Short REM, two wake-ups. You slept 7 h 20 and deep sleep was a solid 1 h 55, but you woke at 04:00 and 06:20, and REM was only 1 h 30. That often follows a hard run late in the evening.

For a better night tonight
  • Your three best nights this month came after morning runs. If you can, move Thursday’s intervals before work.
  • Your REM is longest when you’re asleep by 22:45. Last night it was 23:10.
  • After yesterday’s run your heart rate took almost three hours to settle. On hard days, leave that much time before bed.

Set up in a minute

Add Pons to your AI app once and sign in to Garmin. Then ask on your computer or your phone.

Claude

Every plan works. The Free plan allows one custom connector. Add it on the web or desktop app, and it also works in the phone app.

  1. Open Claude’s “Add custom connector” window, with Pons already filled in.
    Open in Claude
  2. Click Add, then Connect.
  3. Sign in with your Garmin email and password on the Pons page. If Garmin sends you a code, enter it there.
  4. Back in Claude, ask a question.

ChatGPT

Works on the free plan. It takes a short form, once, on chatgpt.com in a browser.

  1. Open ChatGPT’s Plugins page, click Add and choose Add custom MCP server.
  2. Fill in the name Pons, a description such as “Reads my Garmin data”, this address, and OAuth:
    https://pons.jose-maria-martins.workers.dev/mcp
  3. Tick I understand and want to continue, choose Create as a plugin, then install it and sign in to Garmin.
  4. Ask a question. If ChatGPT doesn’t use Pons, type @ and pick it.

Gemini

Not tested yet. Google allows custom apps for personal accounts, 18 or older, in the US, in English, with Keep Activity on.

  1. On a computer, open gemini.google.com, then Settings, Connected Apps, Add a custom app.
  2. Paste the address and leave the advanced fields empty.
    https://pons.jose-maria-martins.workers.dev/mcp
  3. Click Next and sign in to Garmin.

Other apps

Any app that supports remote MCP servers with OAuth can use this address.

A bridge, not a warehouse

Pons carries your data across when you ask, and keeps nothing it doesn’t need. What Pons collects

Read-only
Pons can look at your Garmin data. It can’t change, add or delete anything in your account.
Your password goes only to Garmin
It’s used once to sign you in. Pons keeps Garmin’s sign-in token, encrypted, never the password.
No copy of your health data
Pons reads it from Garmin when your AI asks and passes it straight on.
Leave any time
Ask your AI to “disconnect Pons” and the stored login is deleted.

Questions

Does it work with my watch?

If your Garmin syncs to the Garmin Connect app, Pons can read it. That includes watches, Edge bike computers and Index scales.

Do I need a paid AI plan?

No. Claude’s Free plan allows one custom connector, and ChatGPT works on the free plan too.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Add Pons once on the web or desktop app, and it works in the Claude and ChatGPT phone apps too.

Is Pons free?

Yes. There’s nothing to pay and no Pons account to create.

How fresh is the data?

As fresh as your last sync. Pons reads Garmin Connect, so a workout shows up once your watch has synced with the app.

Can it put a training plan on my watch?

No. Pons only reads. Your AI writes the plan in the chat, and you can add the sessions to your Garmin calendar yourself.

How do I stop using it?

Ask your AI to “disconnect Pons”. That deletes the stored Garmin login. Then remove Pons from your AI app’s connector settings.

I bought my Garmin before running the Lisbon Marathon. It’s on my wrist day and night, so it knows more about me than almost anything I own.

By the second week I was curious: what could I learn if an AI could read all of it? Pons is my answer.
